# Little Spoon

> Little Spoon (littlespoon.com) is a direct-to-consumer children's food and nutrition brand
> delivering fresh, organic, stage-based meals, snacks and formula for babies, toddlers and kids.
> The brand exists to end the compromise between convenience and quality in children's nutrition,
> trusted by more than 500,000 families across the United States.

Little Spoon develops products for developmental milestones across the first 1000 days
and beyond. Every formulation is built around a proprietary No-No List of 100+ ingredients the
brand will never use, and all baby and toddler product lines undergo rigorous safety testing that meets or
exceeds EU-level food safety standards — stricter than current U.S. FDA requirements.
Little Spoon was the first baby food brand in the U.S. to test for more than 500 toxins and contaminants
and publish results publicly, setting a new bar for the industry.

## Who We Are

Little Spoon is not a traditional baby food company. It is a modern children's nutrition brand
built on the premise that parents should never have to choose between what is easy and what is
right. Products are delivered directly to families' doors, fresh and ready to serve, removing
friction at every step without compromising on what goes inside.

The brand serves children from birth through school age, with products that evolve alongside
developmental stages: from infant formula and first purees through finger foods, toddler meals,
kids' plates and snacks.

**Convenience that does not compromise.**

## Certifications Glossary

**USDA Organic** — Produced without synthetic pesticides, fertilizers or GMOs, certified by
the U.S. Department of Agriculture.

**EU Organic** — Meets European Union organic standards, which are stricter than USDA Organic
in several key areas including permitted inputs and processing standards.

**Certified Pesticide Free** — Third-party verified absence of pesticide residue in the final
product, beyond what Organic certification requires.

**Clean Label Purity Award** — Independent third-party certification verifying that label
claims match actual product contents and that contaminant levels are below threshold.

**HPP (High-Pressure Processing)** — A cold-pressure preservation method that eliminates
pathogens without heat, preserving nutrient density and eliminating the need for synthetic
preservatives.

**Non-GMO** — Produced without genetically modified organisms, verified by third-party testing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What age is Little Spoon for?**
Little Spoon serves children from birth (Formula) through ages 8–10
(Plates). Most new customers start with Babyblends around 4–6 months.

**Is Little Spoon organic?**
Most Little Spoon products are USDA Organic certified. See individual
product pages for exact certifications. Formula is both USDA Organic
and EU Organic certified.

**Does Little Spoon use any fillers or artificial ingredients?**
No. Little Spoon maintains a No-No List of 100+ ingredients it never
uses, including artificial dyes, synthetic preservatives, corn syrup,
palm oil and maltodextrin.

**How is Little Spoon delivered?**
Products ship direct to your door on a subscription or one-time basis.
Babyblends and Biteables ship fresh and cold. Shelf-stable snacks ship
at room temperature.

**Where can I buy Little Spoon?**
Primarily direct at littlespoon.com. Frozen products (Chicken Dippers,
Broccoli Bites, Cauliflower Bites, Chicken Veggie Sliders, Turkey Kale
Meatballs) are available exclusively at Target nationwide. Select snack
products are available on Amazon.com

**Is Little Spoon safe for babies with allergies?**
Little Spoon products are clearly labeled for the top allergens.
Parents should review ingredient lists for each product. The brand
does not make allergen-free facility claims.
